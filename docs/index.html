End-of-day data from Yahoo Finance via yfinance (unofficial; split-adjusted, not dividend-adjusted). Avg daily $ volume = average of close × volume over the 20 sessions before the signal day. RSI is Wilder’s 14-day RSI. “vs 20 EMA” is how far the close sits above its 20-day EMA. RS rank is the percentile (0–99) of the 6-month return among all stocks scanned, 99 being the strongest. The 1-month chart has one bar per session (21 sessions): each bar is the return of that day’s close vs the close one month before the last bar, green above that level and red below it.
